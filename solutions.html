Cut Cost on an Existing Cable or Connector Assembly

Any supplier can quote a lower price. Few can systematically find and remove the cost hidden in your BOM, your connector specifications, and your production process. At Gii, cost reduction is how our engineering culture operates — built into every DFM review, every BOM audit, and every supplier negotiation we run on your behalf.

Sounds like you if…

  • You’re paying a premium for branded off-the-shelf connectors
  • One assembly involves several components and several suppliers
  • Leadership has handed you a cost-down target for a program already in production
  • Your volumes have grown, but your unit price hasn’t moved

How Gii helps

  • Design for Manufacturability (DFM) reviews that find real savings
  • Purpose-built custom connectors that replace expensive branded parts
  • BOM optimization — over-specified components and materials identified
  • Component consolidation and make-vs-buy analysis
  • Open-book cost modeling so you see where every dollar goes

Situation 02

Transfer a Program Without Disrupting Production

Transferring a medical device program — from OEM internal production, an outgoing supplier, or another CMO — is one of the most operationally complex things an OEM team can do. Gii has executed these transfers dozens of times. We know where the documentation gaps appear and how to keep production running while a transfer is in progress.

Sounds like you if…

  • Your current supplier is exiting, underperforming, or can’t scale
  • You’re moving a program out of internal production
  • You need the transfer done without a gap in supply
  • Your documentation needs to hold up to change control and audits

How Gii helps

  • Full engineering review of existing processes, tooling, and designs
  • Process revalidation and capability studies at the receiving facility
  • Supply chain remapping and vendor requalification
  • Regulatory documentation support — DHR, DHF, change control
  • First-article inspection and a zero-disruption transfer methodology

Reduce Tariff Exposure and Lead Times

Not every program should be built in the same place. Gii’s footprint lets us match each program to the manufacturing location that delivers the best combination of quality, cost, and risk. In today’s tariff environment, knowing where and how to build is itself a competitive advantage.

Sounds like you if…

  • China tariffs are eroding your margins
  • Long Asia lead times are putting launches or clinical timelines at risk
  • You want your manufacturer in your time zone and a short flight away
  • You need to compare total landed cost, not just unit price

How Gii helps

  • Nearshore manufacturing in Heredia, Costa Rica — same time zone, audit-accessible
  • No China tariff exposure for programs built in Costa Rica
  • Total landed cost modeling — not just unit price
  • Tariff risk assessment and mitigation strategy for your BOM
  • Trade compliance across the US, Costa Rica, and Asia
