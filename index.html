Who We Are

Medical Device Cables & Connectors, Value-Engineered

Global Interconnect, Inc. (Gii) specializes in medical device cables and connectors, offering engineering design and rapid prototyping in the US along with manufacturing in Asia and Central America.

Our focus is energy-based medical devices, including radiofrequency (RF), ultrasonic, laser, and electrosurgical systems. We serve device makers in cardiac electrophysiology, orthopedics, urology, oncology, and pain management.

Our Engineering First focus lets us find value-engineering opportunities generalists miss — which is why leading OEMs come to us for the connectors and cable sub-assemblies inside their most demanding devices.

Proven Results

$7 Million in Savings. One Program. One Partner.

A $5 billion orthopedics OEM was paying a premium they didn't need to. Here's what happened when they brought Gii in.

$7M Cost savings across 500,000 pieces
6 → 2 Components per connector assembly
3 → 1 Suppliers consolidated into Gii
75% Reduction in pin manufacturing cost

The Situation

Their contract manufacturer juggled multiple vendors — connectors, cable, and PCBs sourced separately, then assembled in-house. Complicated, costly, and no one had the expertise to fix it.

What Gii Did

Gii designed a drop-in replacement connector — no OEM capital or equipment changes. Components dropped from six to two, pins moved from machined gold to stamped gold-flash, and we consolidated cable, connectors, and PCBs under one roof — ramping from 1,000 to 20,000 pieces per week.

"Essential orthopedic treatments and procedures are now getting to market faster and more cost-effectively."
