Quality & Compliance

Medical-Grade Quality Systems Behind Every Cable & Connector We Build

For a medical device OEM, a supplier that can’t keep up with your regulatory requirements isn’t a partner — it’s a liability. Gii’s quality systems are built specifically for the documentation burden of medical cable and connector manufacturing, so your interconnect never becomes the weak link in your regulatory file.

Validation & Testing

Zero failure is our standard. Every process is validated and every assembly is tested before it ships — nothing leaves unless it meets your exact specification.

  • IQ/OQ/PQ validation protocols
  • Process capability studies at the manufacturing facility
  • First-article inspection and lot acceptance procedures
  • 100% electrical test on every assembly
  • Environmental monitoring and controls in clean room production

Traceability & Documentation

Our quality management system captures the records your regulatory submissions depend on — from raw material to shipment.

  • Full material traceability from raw material to shipment
  • Device history records (DHR) for every build
  • DHF documentation support for your design history file
  • Robust change control that doesn’t slow production
  • Validation documentation for regulatory submissions

Audit-Ready Facilities
